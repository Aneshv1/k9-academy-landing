A working apprenticeship where you spend 8 days inside our facility working with real dogs every single day across every breed and temperament, from puppies to severe aggression, alongside trainers who've done this 10,000+ times.

## Tone & Voice Guidelines
- Direct, confident, no fluff
- Write like a real person, not a marketer and definitely not like AI
- NEVER use choppy short fragments separated by periods (e.g. "15 years. 10,000+ dogs. Award-winning.") - this is the most obvious AI tell
- NEVER use em dashes - use commas or rewrite the sentence
- No "world-class", "unlock", "embark", "journey", "elevate", "cutting-edge", "game-changing" or any other AI buzzwords
- Don't claim they'll "become a professional in 8 days" - that's misleading, they learn how to become one
- Write in complete, natural sentences that flow like conversation
- The Hormozi sales framework is fine, the persuasion structure works, just make the words sound human
- The business side is a key differentiator, most programs skip it
- Prestigious, not exclusive
- Limited spots, application-based, we actually review them
